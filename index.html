🎤 Beatbox Mic Recorder

Beatbox into the mic, then hit Analyze → Tracks: hits are classified by spectral character — low “boom/puh” → Kick, mid “kah/ts” → Snare, high sizzle “tss” → Hi-Hat — and laid onto the sequencer, exactly like analyzeBeatboxToTracks. No mic? Use the demo take.
